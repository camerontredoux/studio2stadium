# Textarea

A multi-line note field, inset on `bg-300`, at most 640px wide and resizable vertically.

- Used for a coach's private note under a dancer row. Body text is 13px/1.45.
- Focus lifts the border to `line-focus` and adds the `fg` focus ring.
- Consumer provides: `value` or `defaultValue`, `onChange`, `placeholder`, an `aria-label`.
