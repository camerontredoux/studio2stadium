# Accordion

Questions and answers for the coach and dancer FAQ, one under another in a panel.

- Questions are 48px rows in 13.5px/500 with a caret at the right that turns and brightens when open. Answers are `body-long` in `fg-2`.
- Keep answers to a few sentences and at most 760px wide.
- Consumer provides: `items` (`{q, a}`), `open` (indices), `onToggle`.
